Privacy Policy

Document Studio – PDF & Office

Effective date: November 11, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 11, 2026
Published by AstraCore Studio  ·  Android app package: com.documentstudio.pdfoffice

1The short version

  • Document Studio processes your documents on your device. We do not operate a server, do not require an account, and do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers or crash reports.
  • Your files are never uploaded to us.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • The only network use is the optional local-network PC transfer (Pro) and a one-time on-device translation model download (Pro).

2Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • We do not collect your documents, their contents, thumbnails or OCR text.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

3Information stored on your device

The app stores the following in its private storage:

  • Documents you import or create, and their thumbnails.
  • Library metadata: file names, folders, tags, favourites, last read page, bookmarks, annotations and OCR text.
  • Settings: theme, language, app-lock preference and onboarding state.
  • A local Pro entitlement flag after a successful purchase.

This data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it. Files you exported to shared storage or another app are outside the app's storage and are not removed when the app is uninstalled.

4Purchases

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play Billing. Google receives the purchase information needed to complete the transaction under its own privacy policy. The app receives only the product ID, purchase state and the localised price. We do not receive your payment card details. Google's handling of purchase data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5Network access

The app uses the network only in these cases:

  • PC transfer (optional, Pro). You can start a local web server on your phone to send or receive files from a computer on the same Wi-Fi network. The server is protected by a PIN, runs only while the transfer screen is open, and stops after ten minutes of inactivity. Files travel directly between your phone and your computer; they are not sent to us.
  • Translation model download (optional, Pro). The first time you translate a language pair, Google Play services downloads the on-device model. The text you translate is processed by that model on your device and is not uploaded by this app.

OCR, barcode scanning, QR generation, PDF and Office processing, compression, redaction, backup encryption and workflows do not use the network.

7Security

  • Imported files are copied into app-private storage.
  • Encrypted backups use AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your password via PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (210,000 iterations). Your password is never stored.
  • PDF password protection uses the PDF standard security handler. The app cannot open a protected PDF without the correct password and does not attempt to bypass encryption.
  • Redaction permanently removes the marked content from the output file by rasterising the affected pages and painting opaque boxes.
  • App lock gates access to the app's interface. It does not encrypt document files on disk.
  • Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for app data.

8Children

The app is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9Your choices

  • Delete documents individually or empty the trash.
  • Clear temporary files.
  • Export library metadata as JSON.
  • Delete all app data from Settings.
  • Disable app lock at any time.
  • Revoke camera or notification permission in Android settings.

10Third parties

  • Google Play Billing processes purchases.
  • Google ML Kit provides on-device OCR, translation, language identification and barcode scanning. ML Kit models run on-device; translation models are downloaded by Google Play services.

No other third party receives data from the app. These services may process limited technical information under Google's own privacy policy.

11Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date will be updated and the new version will be published at the same URL. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

5网络访问

应用仅在以下情况使用网络：

7安全
